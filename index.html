Common Questions

Everything you need to know before signing up.

Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes — you can upgrade anytime. Visit the front desk or contact staff. Remaining days on your current plan are prorated.
Do I need to book a trainer session in advance?
Walk-ins are always welcome for general use. For personal trainer sessions (included in Standard and above), booking ahead is recommended.
What payment methods do you accept?
GCash, Maya, and cash at the front desk. Digital receipts are issued for every transaction.
Is there a registration fee?
No registration fee for any plan. Walk-ins simply pay the ₱100 day rate at the front desk — no forms required.
What happens when my plan expires?
You'll receive a renewal notification before expiry. Staff can process your renewal quickly — just visit the front desk or call ahead.
